One image that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026, drawn as an editorial front page: NVIDIA reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after he resigned, its shares jumping about 33% as the S&P 500 surged 4.82%, as the lead story, with Ukraine's sinking of the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, sweeping new US sanctions on Iran's shadow-banking network, the Democrats' largest US House majority since 1992, the Dymer apartment strike toll reaching at least 11, UN chief Guterres urging greater AI regulation at Bridgetown, fighting in Ituri disrupting DR Congo's Ebola response, the first Trump-Xi-Putin three-way summit in Shenzhen, and a ticker of the day's smaller stories. draw.py renders the page with PIL from the day's verified headlines; main.py validates the finished PNG (signature, dimensions, size, chunk CRCs) and copies it to the output directory, byte for byte, so repeated runs land on exactly the same file.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes entry.png to /output, or to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR when that is set
```
